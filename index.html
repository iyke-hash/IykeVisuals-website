The honest part about AI video: it is powerful but not perfect. Faces, hands and small text can sometimes look off, and exact logos or product labels in motion are the hardest things to get right. I review every shot and regenerate until it is polished. If something you want is not reliable with AI, I’ll tell you before we start and suggest a workaround, such as placing the exact label as a clean overlay.

Revisions: a round means one consolidated list of changes sent together. Each round is turned around within 24 hours for Tier 1 videos and within 2 days for longer ones. A new concept after script approval, or a rebuild of most of the video, counts as a new project.

Deposits are non-refundable once work has started, because the planning and generation costs are already spent. If your scope changes, I send a written quote before doing any extra work.

How fast will I get my work?

Delivery times are production days. They start once I have your deposit, a complete brief and, for videos, your approved script, and they pause while I wait for your feedback or materials.

Can I pay in dollars?

Yes. Dollar prices apply to payments made in dollars and sit beside the naira prices. Use the ₦ / $ switch in the pricing section.

Can I see examples first?
